# Data attribution

## Atlas schematic map

- World land shape: `ne_110m_land.geojson` from the Natural Earth Vector GeoJSON repository (https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson), downloaded on 2026-09-30. Only its land geometry is used to draw coastlines.
- World marker coordinates: `LABEL_X` / `LABEL_Y` label points from `ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson` (https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson), downloaded on 2026-09-29. Only point coordinates and Chinese country names are used; no country outlines are included.
- Natural Earth data is public domain. Terms of Use: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.
- China is shown as a 34-tile schematic arrangement; no provincial geometry is used.

本站地图为示意图，不表示国界、省界，也不用于划界或导航。
